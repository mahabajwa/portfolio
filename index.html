About Me

I'm Maha Abid, a BSCS student at University of the Punjab with a deep interest in UI/UX design. I believe great design is invisible — when users don't have to think about how to use something, that's when you know the design worked.

I work primarily in Figma, where I've designed 3 complete projects. My process starts with understanding the user, then wireframing, prototyping, and polishing every detail until it feels just right.

As a fresher, I'm eager to learn, grow, and contribute to design teams that care about making things better for real people.
