Case Study

Comparify

A smart shopping price comparison platform — designed to help users find the best deals without visiting multiple stores.

Figma UI Design Final Year Project

The Problem

Online shoppers often have to open many different websites just to check the price of the same product, which takes a lot of time. On top of that, users don't always know the exact name of a product to search for it by text — sometimes they've only seen a photo of it, with no easy way to find where to buy it or compare its price.

My Process

I started by thinking about how a user would search for a product and what they would want to see when comparing prices. I planned the main steps: choose a category, search a product by photo or text, view results from multiple stores, and compare prices in one place. After building the platform, I documented the full design in Figma, mapping out each screen and how they connect.

Design Decisions

I kept the results page simple, with clear sorting options (High to Low, Low to High, and Top Rated), so users could compare products quickly without feeling overwhelmed. I used clean, minimal colors so the focus stayed on the products and prices, not on the design itself.

Final Screens

Outcome & Learning

Working on Comparify taught me how to think from a user's point of view especially when designing the results page, which needed to show a lot of information (prices, ratings, sorting) without feeling cluttered. I also learned how to plan a project from start to finish, from mapping out the user flow to documenting every screen clearly. This project helped me understand that good design isn't just about how something looks, but how easily a user can get what they need.
