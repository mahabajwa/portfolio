Case Study

PageTurn

A book discovery website that helps readers browse by category and continue reading from where they left off after signing in.

Figma UI Design Prototyping

The Problem

Readers often find it hard to discover books that match their interests without scrolling through long, unorganized lists. There's also no easy way for a reader to pick up where they left off in a book they were already reading they have to search for it again every time.

My Process

I started by thinking about how a reader browses for books usually by category rather than searching for a specific title. I planned the main pages: a homepage with categories, and a book listing page showing books within each category. Selecting a book takes the reader straight into reading it, keeping the flow short and simple. After planning this flow, I documented the full design in Figma.

Design Decisions

I organized the homepage around categories first, since that's how most readers naturally browse. I kept the book detail pages simple, focusing on the cover, description, and a clear 'Start Reading' action. I used calming, neutral colors so the focus stayed on the books themselves, not on the interface.

Final Screens

Outcome & Learning

This project helped me think about how to design a simple, natural browsing experience — organizing books by category instead of assuming readers always know what they're searching for. I also learned how to keep a user flow short by removing unnecessary steps, like going straight from selecting a book to reading it, rather than adding extra pages in between.
